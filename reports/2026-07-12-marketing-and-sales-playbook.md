# Marketing & Sales Playbook — getting customers for the prompts

Date: 2026-07-12. Builds ON the master file's existing selling system (§7) and
growth tactics (§8) — this adds what those don't cover: getting outside traffic
and first customers with no audience. Data only; Ali executes after launch.

## What the master file already has (don't re-do)
Sell outcomes not features; pain-question listing opener; trust bio; delta
preview images; review replies in 24h; free lead-magnet; referral 0% after 5
sales; post delta screenshots 2x/week; Select enrollment; bundles; Gumroad;
email list by day 60.

## What the research ADDS (the gaps)

### 1. On-platform SEO — highest-leverage, free, immediate
- PromptBase has built-in buyer traffic; the listing title + description ARE the
  Google/search snippet. Load them with the exact keywords buyers type.
- Validate demand: type a keyword into PromptBase search — if it autocompletes,
  there's demand. Target sub-topics with UNDER ~1,000 existing prompts to avoid
  the most saturated fights.

### 2. Community value-posting — the biggest OUTSIDE lever for a finance niche
- Go where DIY investors already gather: Reddit (r/investing, r/stocks,
  r/SecurityAnalysis), investing Discord servers, finance X/Twitter, LinkedIn.
- Post genuine VALUE, not ads: share a real, useful company breakdown you made
  WITH your own prompt, answer beginners' questions, be helpful. Let people
  discover your profile/product from the quality. Spam gets banned; value earns
  trust. (This is the "Three Rings" method — start closest to the problem.)

### 3. Reviews-first (Ali's own #1 finding: trust converts, not traffic)
- The free mini-prompt is a review magnet: give it away, ask honest users for a
  review. The first 5–10 reviews matter more than any traffic.

### 4. Email list from DAY 1 (earlier than the master file's day-60)
- Social platforms are rented; the email list is owned. Even a free mini-prompt
  in exchange for an email starts it. Fastest way to turn interest into a
  repeat sale.

### 5. Creator partnerships / joint bundles (later)
- Team up with another small finance-content creator: bundle products, each
  promotes to their people. Free reach.

## Honest answer on PAID ADVERTISING (Ali asked "how to advertise")
DO NOT run paid ads now. At $3.99–$5.99 per sale with ~5–10% conversion, a paid
click ($0.50–$2+ on Google/Meta) costs more than the sale earns — you'd lose
money on every customer. Paid ads only work once there's a FUNNEL (email list +
bundles + higher-priced offers) to recoup the cost over time. For now: 100%
organic (free) marketing. Save the money. Revisit paid ads only after there's a
bundle and an email list.

## The catch
Every tactic here activates only AFTER launch. You cannot post a delta
screenshot of, get a review on, or SEO-rank a product that isn't listed. This
playbook is worth knowing before launch — and worth nothing until the account
exists.
