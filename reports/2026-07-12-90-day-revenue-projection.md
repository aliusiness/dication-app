# 90-Day Revenue Projection — Honest Estimate

Date: 2026-07-12. Method: apply the Honest Analyst standard to Ali's own
business — real benchmarks, ranges not point-estimates, assumptions stated,
uncertainty flagged. This is a PROJECTION, not a promise. Save it and compare
against real sales data (the next data we collect).

## Assumptions
- 2–3 products live (Honest Analyst, Earnings Decoder, possibly Tutor).
- Launch price $3.99 → $5.99 after ~10 reviews.
- New seller: no audience, no reviews, no sales history at start.
- PromptBase keeps 20% (Ali keeps 80%); 0% fees via referral link after first
  5 sales. Net per sale ≈ $3.20–$5.00 depending on price and referral.
- Discovery is brutal for new listings ("19-views lesson"); conversion is
  driven by TRUST (reviews/stars), not traffic — Ali's own field finding.

## Real benchmarks
- Top-40 sellers: ~$200–400/month AFTER fees (these are the best, established).
- Consistent new sellers: ~10–20 sales and ~$50–200/month run-rate BY month 3.
- Master file's own stated prize for day 90: "proof + skills + first internet
  money, NOT wealth."

## Three scenarios — TOTAL net over 3 months
- Pessimistic (no traction, no marketing): 0–5 sales → **$0–$20 total.**
  Very possible for a brand-new seller who lists and waits.
- Realistic (steady launch effort, first few reviews, some posting): 12–25
  sales → **~$45–$100 total.** Month-3 run-rate ~$20–$50/month.
- Optimistic (a listing catches + active review-building + social posts):
  30–65 sales → **~$120–$300 total.** Month-3 run-rate ~$80–$200/month.

## Honest headline
Most likely outcome for 90 days: a few tens of dollars, possibly up to a few
hundred if things go well. NOT wealth. Anyone promising thousands in 90 days
from a standing start is lying. This matches the master file's own expectation.

## The variable that dominates everything
Reviews/trust. And it starts at ZERO. Every number above multiplies by zero
until the seller account exists and the first listing is live. The projection
is not the point — LAUNCHING is, because launching is what turns any of these
numbers from hypothetical into real.

## The real 90-day ROI (bigger than the money)
Skills already built: prompt design, two-engine testing, versioning/changelog,
market research, honest copywriting. Plus proof Ali can finish and ship. Those
compound into the next business; $50 does not. The money is the receipt, not
the reward.
