# PromptBase Platform Rules — Research Notes

Date: 2026-07-11
Source: Ali's research on PromptBase seller mechanics

## ⚠️ Step zero — before building anything on the account

PromptBase pays sellers through Stripe, and Stripe is not supported in every country.
The FIRST action when opening the seller account: verify Ali's country appears in the
payout list during signup. If it does not: solve payment first (their notification list,
their alternative provider, the lighter "Stripe Express" account type, or an account
through a trusted family member abroad) — before creating any listings.

## Payout rules

- Seller keeps 80%. It lands in a pending balance and becomes available after 72 hours.
- New sellers are paid monthly; after 50 sales you can switch to weekly.
- Minimum payout: $30.
- Early-payout option: $3.99 fee, available once the account is 30 days old.
- Conclusion Ali drew: first money arrives on a schedule — patience is part of the
  design, not a bad sign.

## Free trust tools

- Seller rank is built from views, sales, and favorites.
- Model badges arrive at 2 sales; category badges at 5 sales in one category —
  which supports focusing on one category ("one shelf") at launch.
- Upload streak: an unbroken chain of daily actions. One submission per day keeps it
  alive — useful during launch week.
- Free-prompts library exists: one small free mini-prompt can become a zero-cost
  review magnet pointing at the paid products.

## Confirmed rejection reasons

Listings get refused when outputs are inconsistent or when samples don't match the
promise. Ali's tested-screenshots rule (only publish with real, tested output
screenshots) is exactly the approval strategy.
