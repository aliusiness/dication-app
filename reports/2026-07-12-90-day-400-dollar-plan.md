# 90-Day Plan — Target $400

Date: 2026-07-12. Goal: $400 net over 90 days from the Honest Analyst prompt
business. Honest, aggressive, and built around the REAL levers, not the
comfortable ones.

## First — is $400 realistic?
It is a STRETCH GOAL (a target set higher than what's likely, to push effort).
Typical new sellers make ~$50–$150 over 90 days. $400 is roughly 2–4× that —
top-decile performance. Possible, NOT guaranteed: the market decides, effort
only raises the odds. This plan maximizes the odds. If it lands at $150, that is
still a real win, above typical — do not quit if $400 doesn't hit exactly.

## The math of $400 (why bundles matter)
- Singles only at ~$4 net → need ~100 sales. Very hard.
- Realistic mix: ~40 singles (~$160) + ~20 bundles at ~$12 net (~$240) = $400.
- Lesson: bundles and higher prices carry the goal, not raw sale count.

## The honest truth about effort — read this twice
More prompts do NOT equal more money. TRUST (reviews) converts buyers, not
product count — Ali's own #1 finding. So working 12 hours/day GENERATING PROMPTS
is the wrong lever; it feels productive but barely moves sales. The money lever
is MARKETING + REVIEWS. If Ali works long hours, most go to marketing, not
building. Building caps at ~1 tested product/week; everything else is selling.

## Time recommendation
Consistency beats intensity. ~4–6 focused hours/day, EVERY day for 90 days,
beats 12-hour days that burn out in two weeks. Sustainable > heroic.

## Daily engine (the habits that produce the $400)
1. 1–2 genuine VALUE posts in investor communities (Reddit r/investing r/stocks,
   finance Discord, finance X) — real breakdowns made with the prompt, not ads.
   THIS is the traffic lever.
2. Reply to every review/comment within 24h.
3. 30 min learning: study top sellers' listings, improve titles/previews.
4. ~1 hr/week (not per day) building + testing ONE new product via the meta-prompt.

- Double down on the ONE product + ONE community that works; drop what doesn't.

## Biggest risks
1. Wrong-lever trap: spending the hours making prompts instead of marketing.
2. Burnout from 12-hour days — pace for 90 days, not 9.
3. Quitting if $400 doesn't hit — the real prize is skills + proof + first money;
   $150 is still a win and a reason to continue, not stop.
