# Simple 3-Month Plan to Sell More

Plain and easy. Do the steps in order. Each month has one goal.

## MONTH 1 — GET LIVE + FIRST REVIEWS
Goal: put your products in the shop and get your first few reviews.
1. Open the PromptBase seller account. (Nothing else works until this is done.)
2. List product #1 (Honest Analyst) at $3.99. Next day, list product #2
   (Earnings Decoder). One per day.
3. Put ONE small FREE prompt in the shop. Free things pull people in and bring
   reviews at no cost.
4. When someone buys, reply kindly and ask: "If it helped, an honest review
   means a lot." 
Win for the month: 3 listings live, 1–3 first sales, first reviews.

## MONTH 2 — TELL PEOPLE (free marketing)
Goal: bring visitors and reach 10 reviews.
1. Twice a week, take a real company, run it through your prompt, and post the
   useful result in investor groups (Reddit r/investing and r/stocks, finance
   Discord, finance X/Twitter). HELP people. Do not spam.
2. Your profile/shop is visible on your posts — people find you from the quality.
3. Keep answering every review within one day.
4. When you reach about 10 reviews, raise the price to $5.99.
Win for the month: ~10 reviews, price raised, steady small sales.

## MONTH 3 — GROW WHAT WORKS
Goal: earn more from the same work.
1. Put your prompts together as one bundle ("The Honest Analyst System") for
   $9.99–$14.99. A bundle earns more per buyer.
2. Give the free prompt in exchange for an email — this starts your email list
   (people you can tell about new products later).
3. Keep posting value twice a week.
4. Look at your numbers: which prompt sells most? Do more like that one.
Win for the month: first bundle sold, email list started.

## The one rule
Be CONSISTENT — do the weekly posting every week, not once. Small actions,
repeated, are what work. Start each step now, not "later."

## The honest truth
This whole plan earns $0 until Month 1, Step 1 is done: the account. Everything
else is built and waiting.
