# Ali's AI Automation Business — Step-by-Step Plan

Tailored to: no coding experience (use no-code tools + Claude as helper/teacher),
strong prompting skill, MacBook Pro M5, crypto payment ready (Solana USDC wallet),
young, English improving, restricted country (international clients pay crypto).

Business in one line: build AI systems that do businesses' repetitive work
(answering emails, booking appointments, writing posts), charge a setup fee +
a monthly retainer. Paid in USDC to the Solana wallet.

## PHASE 1 — Learn ONE tool (Week 1)
- Master **Make.com** — the easiest no-code automation tool for beginners.
  Free tier = 1,000 operations/month, enough to learn and demo.
- Learn the basics: triggers (what starts an automation), actions (what it does),
  and how to connect AI (ChatGPT/Claude) into a workflow.
- Free learning: Make's own Academy + YouTube ("Make.com ChatGPT automation").
- Use Claude to explain anything confusing, and Claude Code for any custom step.
- Goal: build one tiny AI workflow yourself by end of week.

## PHASE 2 — Build 3 demo automations = your PORTFOLIO (Week 2)
Build these for a pretend business so you have proof you can do it:
1. AI email responder — reads an incoming email, AI drafts a reply.
2. AI social-post generator — you give a topic, AI writes posts and schedules them.
3. AI lead handler — a form submission → AI sorts it and sends a personal reply.
Record a short screen video of each one working. That's your proof.

## PHASE 3 — Pick your NICHE + offer (Week 2–3)
- Pick ONE type of business that has repetitive work AND money to pay $200+/mo:
  dental/medical clinics, real-estate agents, e-commerce stores, gyms/salons.
  (Clinics are a top pick — AI can handle bookings and reminders.)
- One clear offer: "I build an AI system that [answers your customer emails /
  books appointments] automatically."
- Pricing:
  - First 1–2 clients: cheap ($100–$500 setup) to earn testimonials.
  - After proof: $1,000–$3,000 setup + $200–$500/month retainer.
  - By client 5–6: $5,000+ projects + retainers.
- Payment: USDC to the Solana wallet → cash out via P2P to Mashreq.

## PHASE 4 — Get your first client (Week 3–6) — the hard, important part
- Daily: send 15–25 targeted messages to businesses in your niche
  (LinkedIn, email, Instagram/Facebook).
- Best tactic (~40% reply rate): find a business with a slow or broken process,
  BUILD a quick demo using THEIR situation, and send a short video: "I made this
  for your clinic — it books appointments automatically. Want it?"
- Offer the first one cheap or free in exchange for a testimonial.
- Deliver → get the testimonial → use it to land the next.

## PHASE 5 — Grow (ongoing)
- Every happy client → testimonial → referrals, and raise your prices.
- Reuse the same automation for many businesses in the same niche.

## Costs & timeline
- Startup cost: ~$0–$100 (Make free tier, AI API costs cents, optional domain later).
- Timeline with daily outreach: first discovery call in 1–2 weeks, first paying
  client in 4–8 weeks.

## The one honest truth
The hard part is Phase 4 — sending messages every single day. Most people quit
there. The ones who do the daily outreach win. Building the automations is the
easy, fun part; getting clients is the real job.

## Your edges
Strong prompting skill (the AI parts will be excellent), Claude as your coding
teacher, a powerful M5 Mac, and a crypto wallet already set up for payment.

## Start TODAY
Make a free Make.com account and watch one "Make.com + ChatGPT automation"
YouTube tutorial. Build one tiny automation today — that's the first finish.
