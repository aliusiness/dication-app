## RULES (from Belfort, Harvey Specter, Daniel Priestley, cold-call video)
- Lead with real INFORMATION/pain, not "hi how are you." Give them a reason to
  stay in the first 10 seconds.
- Never lie or invent a hook (no fake bug reports, no fake "I called you").
- Be sharp, confident, expert. State the fix exists; don't over-ask permission.
- Let the customer feel free — they pick the day, don't hard-set a time.
- SPEED TO LEAD: reply within 1 minute if they respond ("money loves speed").
- FOLLOW UP up to 7 times before giving up — most sales come after the first
  message is ignored.
- When they reply, LISTEN FIRST (fill 3 baskets: their situation, their goal,
  their problems) before pitching. Don't answer objections one-by-one — gather
  them all, then respond.
- Order of pitching: Insights -> Methods -> Solution (never jump to the product).
- Offer tiers later: Bronze / Silver / Gold pricing.

## PRICING (first clients)
